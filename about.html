About Granitebrook

Building trust, one project at a time

A family-run contractor built on straightforward pricing, skilled crews, and finishing what we start.

Our story

From one truck to a full-service builder

Granitebrook started as a two-person framing crew and grew by doing one thing well: treating every client's project like it was our own home.

Today our team of carpenters, roofers, electricians, plumbers, and project managers handles everything from single-room remodels to multi-million-dollar commercial builds. We have kept the same approach we started with — show up when we say we will, explain the work in plain language, and stand behind it after the final invoice.

How it works

Our four-step process

A clear path from first conversation to final walkthrough.

Consultation

We visit the site, listen to your goals, and discuss budget, timeline, and design options.

Proposal

You receive a detailed, fixed-price proposal with scope, schedule, and payment milestones.

Build

Permits pulled, crews scheduled, and weekly photo updates sent until the work is complete.

Walkthrough

We review every detail together, complete the punch list, and hand over warranty documents.
