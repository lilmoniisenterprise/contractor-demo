Who we are

A builder you can actually reach

Granitebrook Builders is a family-run general contractor serving homeowners, developers, and businesses across Ashford County.

Every project gets a dedicated project manager, a clear written scope, and a schedule you can see. We self-perform framing, finish carpentry, and roofing, and we coordinate licensed electrical and plumbing crews in-house — so fewer handoffs, fewer surprises.

  • Fixed-price proposals with line-item detail
  • Weekly progress updates with photos
  • Permits, inspections, and code compliance handled for you
  • Clean, safe, respectful jobsites
